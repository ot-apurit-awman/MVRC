Version 0.6.0
- Fixed a huge issue that caused performance to drop alot. Game feels now much smoother again.
- Pickable objects can finally be thrown around.
- Menu can now be used from VR.
- Added a laser pointer to the right hand that is used to navigate pause menu and also ordering food from PSK. 
- Made sauna ladle usable in VR (trigger picks up water and water is thrown by doing the right click related action)
- Made spray canisters usable in VR.
- Things like catalog and other papers appear now on the left hand and can be interacted with the laser pointer.
- Attached the wristwatch permanently to the left hand.

As before this update will likely bring new bugs. I will try to fix them when i can.

Version 0.5.0
- You can now rebind VR controls in SteamVR settings
- Added fueling in VR
- Seatbelts can now be used with VR hands
- Sprint is now a toggle, so you can click once to sprint and click again to stop sprinting
- You can now recenter the view with a controller button in addition to the keyboard recenter key
Fixed
- Being drunk now correctly changes the VR view as it does in the game, including the wobbly fisheye effect
- The right-hand punch pose now works for breaking glass and hitting people
- MiddleFinger hand pose works as well
- Grabbing, picking up, and dropping objects is more reliable during normal gameplay
- The HUD is now on the underside of your left arm so stats are easier to check in VR

- Grabbing for some reason doesn't work unless you left click atleast once in game using the mouse.
- Likely includes lots of bugs caused by the new interaction system. Report bugs on the mod page so i can try to fix them
